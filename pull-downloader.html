Questions

Short answers.

Which sites work?

YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, Pinterest and X. More platforms are added over time.

Is it free?

Yes. Downloads are free with no account.

Can I download any video?

Only download content you own or have permission to use. Respect each platform’s terms and the creator’s copyright.

Where do files go?

They save to your device’s downloads folder, like any other file.
